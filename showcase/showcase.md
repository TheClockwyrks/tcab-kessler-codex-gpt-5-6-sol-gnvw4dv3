# Kessler

Kessler is a score-chasing orbital demolition game played above a luminous, lonely world. Sweep your deflector around its low orbit, launch the demolition ball, and steer every return into three crowded rings of abandoned machinery.

Each wave asks you to break every derelict without letting the last ball burn up in the atmosphere. The inner structures are brittle, the armored middle ring needs another strike, and the outer wreckage is richest—but the rings soon counter-rotate fast enough to turn every gap into a moving target.

Destroyed targets sometimes shed salvage. Catch it to widen the deflector, split new balls into orbit, raise a one-hit shield, or punch straight through armor. Not every recovery helps: a narrow pod shrinks your reach, forcing a tense change of rhythm until its timer expires.

The field grows faster forever. Read the arcs, shape the bounce, and keep the sky clean.
